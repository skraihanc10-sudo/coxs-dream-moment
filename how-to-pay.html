Decoration Packages / How to pay

How to pay

A 20% advance holds your date. The rest is paid on the day, once you have seen the setup.

How it works

  1. Choose a package and send the booking Tell us the date and how to reach you. Nothing is charged at this point.
  2. Send the 20% advance To one of our own accounts below, then attach the screenshot. This is what holds your date.
  3. Pay the rest on the day After you have seen the setup on the beach. Cash, bKash, whichever suits you.

Where to send it

Make sure you are paying us

Questions people ask

  1. What if it rains? The date moves at no cost. The advance carries over.
  2. Can I change the date? Free up to 48 hours before. Within 24 hours the advance is not refundable, because the flowers and the cake are already bought.
  3. Do I have to pay the advance online? No. Message us and we will arrange it however suits you — but a date is only held once the advance is in.
